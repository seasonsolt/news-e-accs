Self-contained headline rules:
1. Name the central model, product, company or project and its action or result.
2. Keep or lightly edit a clear English original; translate other languages directly into English.
3. A version-only title or teaser needs its subject from the original body or verified source context. Never infer a missing model/version from industry knowledge.
4. Preserve the original article type and central action. How/Why/Guide/Analysis/Review/Benchmark articles remain explanations, guides or reviews; discussion of an existing product does not become a launch announcement.
