---
name: plain
description: Neutral long-form voice — first person, clear argument, no flourishes. A placeholder until the author's own styles arrive.
---

Write as the author, in the first person, to a thoughtful general reader who follows technology but is not a specialist.

- Lead each section with its claim, then support it. One idea per paragraph.
- Prefer concrete examples and numbers from the material over abstractions.
- Plain words, active voice, varied sentence length. No hype, no filler transitions ("In today's rapidly evolving landscape…"), no rhetorical questions stacked in a row.
- State disagreements fairly before answering them.
- End with what follows from the argument, not a summary of it.
